Further Reading

Sadly, the rise of Irish whiskey over the past decade has not been matched by literary releases. Many of the books you will find on the topic were written more than a decade ago, with a heavy focus on its history rather than its current state and the stories that go with its meteoric rise. I hope that the journalistic and cultural merit of documenting Irish whiskey's story is recognised by another generation of writers. There is much to tell.

Here is a selection of reads that have informed and inspired me over the last decade. Happy reading.

A Glass Apart: Irish Single Pot Still Whiskey

Fionnan O'Connor

The definitive tome on pot still, and the reason a good deal of what has happened since happened. Out of print now, with secondhand copies usually carrying the price to match.

Still With Us: Formative Imperatives in the History of Material Irish Whiskey

Fionnan O'Connor

A 2025 TU Dublin PhD thesis covering historical mash bill research, including ten historic recipes distilled at Boann and assessed by a panel of the trade.

From Barley to Blarney: A Whiskey Lover's Guide to Ireland

Sean Muldoon, Jack McGarry and Tim Herlihy

The Dead Rabbit team's field guide to whiskey tourism, covering distilleries, bars and a dozen of their own cocktails at the back.

Written by a writer-turned-distiller, and a great overview of the historical decisions that led Irish whiskey to where it is today.

Irish Whiskey Book FAQ

Questions readers search for.

What are the best Irish whiskey books?

The best Irish whiskey books depend on the reader. Bonded is the current annual handbook, A Glass Apart is important for single pot still whiskey, and The Whiskeys of Ireland gives readers a broad historical and category-led introduction.

Is there a book about Irish whiskey distilleries?

Yes. Bonded profiles every working Irish whiskey distillery and explains each site through clear fact boxes and regional context.

What is a good Irish whiskey gift?

A hardback Irish whiskey book makes a strong gift for collectors, travellers, bartenders and anyone building a deeper understanding of Ireland's national spirit.

Are there books about single pot still Irish whiskey?

Yes. Bonded's first edition focuses on The Year of Single Pot Still, while A Glass Apart remains an important modern reference on the style.
