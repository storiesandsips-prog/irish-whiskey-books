Irish Whiskey Book FAQ

Questions readers search for.

What is the best Irish whiskey book?

For a current, annual view of the category, Bonded is designed as the modern handbook: distilleries, releases, maps, numbers, essays and verified facts in one volume.

Is there a book about Irish whiskey distilleries?

Yes. Bonded profiles every working Irish whiskey distillery and explains each site through clear fact boxes and regional context.

What is a good Irish whiskey gift?

A hardback Irish whiskey book makes a strong gift for collectors, travelers, bartenders and anyone building a deeper understanding of Ireland's national spirit.

Are there books about single pot still Irish whiskey?

Yes. Bonded's first edition focuses on The Year of Pot Still Whiskey, while Fionnán O'Connor's A Glass Apart remains an important modern reference on the style.
