Further Reading

Sadly, the rise of Irish whiskey over the past decade has not been matched by literary releases. Many Irish whiskey books were written more than a decade ago, with a heavy focus on Irish whiskey history rather than its current state and the stories that go with its meteoric rise. I hope that the journalistic and cultural merit of documenting Irish whiskey's story is recognised by another generation of writers. There is much to tell.

Here is a selection of reads that have informed and inspired me over the last decade, from Irish single pot still whiskey and lost distilleries to broader whiskey history, travel and reference. Happy reading.

A Glass Apart: Irish Single Pot Still Whiskey

Fionnan O'Connor

The definitive tome on Irish single pot still whiskey, and the reason a good deal of what has happened since happened. Out of print now, with secondhand copies usually carrying the price to match.

Still With Us: Formative Imperatives in the History of Material Irish Whiskey

Fionnan O'Connor

A 2025 TU Dublin PhD thesis covering Irish whiskey history and historical mash bill research, including ten historic recipes distilled at Boann and assessed by a panel of the trade.

Download free

From Barley to Blarney: A Whiskey Lover's Guide to Ireland

Sean Muldoon, Jack McGarry and Tim Herlihy

The Dead Rabbit team's Irish whiskey travel guide, covering distilleries, bars and a dozen of their own cocktails at the back.

Find in stock

The Whiskeys of Ireland

Peter Mulryan

Written by a writer-turned-distiller, and a great overview of the historical decisions that led Irish whiskey to where it is today.

Bushmills: 400 Years in the Making

Peter Mulryan

Out of print and generally inexpensive secondhand. The only in-depth account of the famed Antrim distillery.

The World Atlas of Whisky

Dave Broom

A tremendously in-depth volume on whiskey globally, with the 2024 third edition bringing the wider world of whisky up to date.

Gary Quinn

A pocket-sized and inexpensive introduction to Irish whiskey, built around short entries on well-known and well-loved bottles.

The Lost Distilleries of Ireland

Distillery by distillery, a record of what Ireland once had and what became of it. Still one of the essential books on vanished Irish whiskey places.

A scholarly tome, long out of print and quite scarce. A copy in good condition will often cost three figures.

The original 1887 journey through the whisky distilleries of the United Kingdom, including twenty-eight Irish distilleries in detail.
